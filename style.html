Style guide

One small set of rules for the whole app. Based on Apple's iOS text styles, an 8-point spacing grid and 44-point tap targets.

1 · Type: 7 sizes instead of 16

Sizes are in rem, so they grow with the iPhone text-size setting (Apple calls this Dynamic Type). Numbers use tabular figures so the countdown doesn't wobble.

2 · Spacing: 8-point grid

${''}

Page edge 16 · card padding 16 · gap between cards 12 · between sections 24–32.

3 · Corners: 3 values, nested

Card 20 · photo/inner box 12 · chips round (999). Inner corner = outer corner − padding, so curves sit parallel (Apple's "concentric" rule).

Road steps keep their own colour and an icon and a word, never colour alone. Raptor changes from near-black (invisible on navy) to pink.

5 · One card anatomy everywhere

❄️ Cryo testsShip 4326 Oct · 16:32 UK

Ship 43 returned to Megabay 2 after cryo testing.

Photo · @LabPadreView on X ↗
  • Top: what (step) · who (vehicle) · when (date, UK).
  • Middle: one sentence, then one photo in the locked 16:9 frame, vehicle centred.
  • Bottom: credit on the left, link on the right. Always in that spot.

6 · Words

  • Credit = who published it. "Photo · @SpaceX", "Photo · @RGVaerialphotos", "Source · NASASpaceflight". Never a vehicle name.
  • Vehicles: always "Booster 22 · Ship 42". No B22/S42 on screen.
  • Dates: "13 Nov 2026" for flights, "26 Oct · 16:32 UK" for updates. Year only when it isn't this year.
  • Status words: Done · In progress · Not yet · Confirmed / Not confirmed.
  • Unknowns: say it once ("Date to be confirmed"), never three times.
  • Live: present tense ("Flight 16 is flying"), past tense once it lands.

7 · Touch and motion

  • Everything tappable is at least 44 × 44 points.
  • One easing curve for everything (0.35 s, iOS-style settle). Cards shrink slightly when pressed.
  • Progress bars fill when a step completes. The live dot pulses only while live.
  • All motion switches off when "Reduce Motion" is on.
