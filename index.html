What should pop, what should recede

1
Live state and countdown. The only red on screen, and only while live. Big, steady digits.
2
Next milestone and progress. "Next: Raptor engines" plus 6 labelled bars. People push harder when they can see the finish line (goal-gradient effect).
3
Latest update. One sentence, one photo, one credit.
4
History. Quieter, compact, grouped by year. Unknowns ("to be confirmed") are said once, softly.

The idea: when one thing looks different from everything around it, that's what you notice and remember (the von Restorff effect), so only one thing gets to look different.

Your call

💚 You'll likely love

  • A top card that changes with the day. It's waiting, then counting down, then live in red ("Flight 15 is flying"), then the result. Road shows 6 of 6 done from liftoff.
  • Road progress you can read. 6 labelled bars and one line ("Done Cryo · In progress Pad · Not yet …") replace the unlabelled coloured dots. It's the same on Page 1, Page 2 and opened cards.
  • Booster vs Ship side by side on Page 2, like Apple's compare-models page.
  • Filter updates by All, Booster 23, Ship 43 or Pad.
  • One photo frame everywhere (landscape, vehicle centred), always credited underneath ("Photo · @SpaceX").
  • Much faster to open. The live Page 1 downloads 17.5 MB of photos; this prototype's whole photo set is 1.3 MB.

🤔 You might not like (and why I'd still try it)

  • iPhone's own font instead of Manrope. It has less personality, but it's sharper at small sizes and grows with your iPhone text setting. Compare both with the toggle in the Style guide.
  • Page 2 shows the newest update first instead of grouping by step. "What's new?" is why you open it. "By step" stays one tap away.
  • Bigger text and taps, so pages get longer. Most text today is 12.5 px; Apple's reading size is 17.
  • Raptor goes pink instead of near-black. You chose black earlier, but on navy it measures 1.2 : 1, which is close to invisible as a dot.
  • A plain page switch instead of the sliding header pill. It loses some playfulness, but the targets are bigger and clearer.
  • Compact log rows with year headings. It feels less like a timeline story, but 15 flights are quicker to scan.

✅ Ready to push now (simple fixes, no prototype needed)

  • Photo credit names who published the photo, not "Booster 22".
  • About stops saying "All photos by SpaceX" and stops showing a stuck "Last checked 14 Nov".
  • Static fires stops showing a vehicle-assignment news story twice.
  • One time format: "26 Oct · 16:32 UK" everywhere.
  • One vehicle name: "Booster 22 · Ship 42", no "B22 / S42".
  • Readable grey text: 2.9 : 1 becomes 4.9 : 1.
  • Bigger taps: everything at least 44 points, with no visual change.
  • No text under 11 px: photo credits are 9.5 px today.
  • "Manage notifications" uses the app font. It's the only text in Arial.
  • "Highlights coming soon" becomes quiet text instead of a dashed box.
  • Photo files shrunk: same pictures, about 13× less data.

Top findings

  1. Credits name vehicles, and About makes a false claim. The opened card's photo corner says "Booster 22 / Ship 42". About says every photo is by SpaceX, but Road photos come from @RGVaerialphotos, @LabPadre and others.app.js vehiclePhotosOf / cardPhotosOf, About line 1318
  2. Static fires shows a news story twice. "SpaceX may fly Flight 16 with Booster 23 and Ship 43" appears under Static fires for both vehicles, while the step itself says "Not yet".road.json feed item news-mwpe749c-226041, box "static"
  3. The newest Pad update is hidden. "Pad 2 work has resumed" (19 Nov) is in the data, but both pages show the 28 Oct item.Its hardware is "Pad 2", not a vehicle
  4. Pad says "Not yet" on Page 1, but the data says in progress and Page 2 shows Pad updates.
  5. Road dots are colour only, with no labels. The Raptor dot is near-black on navy (1.2 : 1).
  6. 17.5 MB of photos load on open. One gallery photo alone is 3.8 MB, and a 100 px thumbnail is 2.9 MB.
  7. There are 16 different text sizes, the smallest 9.5 px (44 places). Apple uses 11 text styles with an 11 pt minimum.
  8. The grey "faint" text is too low-contrast: 2.9 : 1 on cards against a 4.5 : 1 minimum. It's used for "8 days since…", "Version 2" and similar.
  9. Taps under 44 pt: the header buttons (40), "Earlier updates" (14 tall) and the source badge (22).
  10. The same thing gets different words. "26 Oct · 16:32" vs "13 Sep · 05:02 UK", "B22 / S42" vs "Booster 22", "Cryo / Raptor / Pad" vs "Cryo tests / Raptor engines / Pad activity", and "SHIP 43" vs "Ship 43".
  11. The top card says "to be confirmed/announced" three times. About shows "Last checked 14 Nov" even though the checker ran today at 14:45 UK.
  12. Credits come in three styles (avatar badge on the photo, a "Photo · @handle" line, a vehicle name in the corner), and some Page 2 cards show no credit line at all.
